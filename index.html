About

A multidisciplinary creative.

I work across video editing, photo editing, graphic design, cinematography, photography, and trading. Each discipline sharpens the others: framing from the camera shapes my edits, and the discipline of the markets shapes how I work.

My journey started with a camera and curiosity, and grew into a practice built on visual storytelling, close attention to detail, and a commitment to keep improving with every project.

I believe creativity is a continuous process of learning, experimenting, and refining. Every project is an opportunity to push boundaries, develop new skills, and create work with purpose.
